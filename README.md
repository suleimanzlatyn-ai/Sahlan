# SAHLAN Premium Cleaners

A responsive, single-file homepage for SAHLAN Premium Cleaners. It uses the brand's navy, sky-blue and gold palette, animated inline SVG logo, service cards, FAQ section and an on-page booking form.

## Booking behaviour

- The **Book / Quote**, **Request a quote**, and **Book your clean** buttons scroll to the booking form on the homepage.
- Required fields are checked before a WhatsApp message is composed.
- The visitor reviews the message in WhatsApp and presses Send.
- This is a booking request, not an appointment confirmation; SAHLAN confirms availability and pricing directly.
- Contact number: **0711 224 477** (WhatsApp: +254 711 224 477).
- Instagram: [@sahlanpremiumcleaners](https://www.instagram.com/sahlanpremiumcleaners/).

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or package installation is required.

## Before launch

Confirm the business service areas and replace illustrative stock photography with approved company photos when available. Verify the final production domain and test the booking form on a phone.
